About

Aperture is a cinematic photography portfolio built to let images breathe. Each frame is a moment isolated from the noise — rendered here in three dimensions, lit by restraint, and scored only by the quiet mechanics of a lens.

The site treats every photograph as an object in space: the ring on the home page is not a slideshow but a gallery you can walk around. Hover, drag, scroll — the camera follows your intent. This is photography rewritten for the medium that shows it.
